# Protected credit evidence: Step 9B.8.2

Saved HDFC billing accounts open three explicitly chosen evidence views: statement checkpoints, separately dated current reports, and entries. Each view reads retained evidence through the protected credit APIs. No view selects a financial balance, determines payment status, posts a movement, or claims a live issuer connection.

## Acceptance contract

- Open evidence only after an explicit billing-account and report-kind choice. Query bounded pages in reference order; explain that newest stored revisions and reference order do not establish financial freshness, chronological order or complete coverage.
- Preserve exact native cash spelling, signed checkpoints, missing reasons, source-local dates/instants, and absent versus present-missing original entry cash. Display preferences never convert these source values. Mask known, missing, zero and historical values everywhere in rendered content and accessibility attributes.
- Bind every page to account and kind. History additionally binds the retained root and source, advances and honors the highest revision observed in the current inspection flow, and validates contiguous revisions and exact continuation. Exact inspection requests the clicked revision and rejects substituted values or mapping provenance; observed account lifecycle may change independently, and a newest revision may become historical. Exact revision inspection rejects a response that reclassifies the selected historical revision as newest; a previously newest revision may become historical.
- Validate complete finite response shapes inside the existing protected financial projection before showing any row. Reject wrong accounts, kinds, sources, malformed decimals/dates, impossible treatment/pending combinations, extra fields, false pagination and altered immutable evidence.
- Keep only one value page at a time, its selected immutable revision context, and bounded cursor metadata. Loading, failure, navigation, lock, sign-out and changed sign-in discard old private state; late responses cannot restore it. Failure recovery starts with a fresh account directory. Reuse same-origin POST, CSRF, workspace context and active MFA session protection without browser persistence or automatic retry.
- Make source references and interpretation caveats expandable. Support both themes, keyboard focus, desktop/mobile reflow and accessible names without putting values into button names, URLs, document titles or persistent storage.
